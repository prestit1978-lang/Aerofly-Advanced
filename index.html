02 / SUPPORT REGISTRATION

Your aircraft.

Every user can request a unique support key at no cost. Keys help identify users and their aircraft version when requesting technical help. A support key is not a purchase or hardware lock.

Free key requests

A secure self-service registration service is being prepared. Until it is connected, support keys are issued manually by the project owner.
