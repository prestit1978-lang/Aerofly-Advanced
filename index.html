02 / REGISTRATION & SUPPORT

Registration & technical support

Each user will receive a unique support key to identify their aircraft version and simplify technical support. Registration is provided at no charge and is not tied to your computer hardware.

Support key requests

The registration service is being prepared. A private registration process is being prepared. Once available, the request page will issue an individual support ID and key for each aircraft.

Example key: A35X-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX

Please do not share personal details or support keys in public GitHub issues.
